Dear Editors of Systems & Control Letters,

Please consider the manuscript "Beyond path-structured barrier certificates: exact embeddings, degree separation, and verified SOS synthesis" by Reza Iraji, Vishnu Murali, and Majid Zamani.

The paper studies when vector and globally scaled interpolation-inspired barrier certificates are interchangeable and when coupling structure changes the minimum polynomial degree. It proves a bidirectional correspondence for the anchored path-matrix subclass and transports SOS witnesses without another optimization. A finite-order obstruction rules out affine globally scaled IBCs of any finite length for a class of linear transitions, while cyclic affine vector certificates prove safety. This is a strict template-level result, not an inference from failed numerical searches.

The Julia artifact uses genuine semidefinite SOS synthesis and independent exact-rational replay. It includes a corrected analysis of two polynomial examples, a source-matched external task, an explicitly labeled logistic-map adaptation, scalar baselines, and exact two- and four-dimensional structural witnesses. The limits of these comparisons are stated explicitly.

We believe the theorem-led analysis and its safety-verification implications are relevant to the readership of Systems & Control Letters.

Sincerely,
Reza Iraji
On behalf of the authors
