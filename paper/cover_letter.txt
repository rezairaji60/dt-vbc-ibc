Dear Editors of Systems & Control Letters,

Please consider the manuscript "Beyond path-structured barrier certificates: exact embeddings, degree separation, and verified SOS synthesis" by Reza Iraji, Vishnu Murali, and Majid Zamani.

The paper studies when vector and interpolation-inspired barrier certificates are interchangeable and when their coupling structure changes the minimum polynomial degree. It proves a bidirectional correspondence for the robust anchored globally scaled path-matrix subclass, including transport of SOS witnesses without another optimization. Separately, a finite-order orbit-average obstruction rules out affine implication-style IBCs of any finite length for a class of linear transitions, while cyclic affine vector certificates prove safety. Quadratic repeated frames exist, making the degree separation tight. This distinction holds before an SOS relaxation is imposed.

The Julia artifact combines genuine semidefinite SOS synthesis with independent exact-rational replay. It includes corrected polynomial examples, a source-matched external task, an explicitly labeled logistic-map adaptation, scalar baselines, and exact two- and four-dimensional structural witnesses. The limits of these comparisons are stated explicitly.

We believe the theorem-led analysis and its safety-verification implications are relevant to Systems & Control Letters.

Sincerely,
Reza Iraji
On behalf of the authors
